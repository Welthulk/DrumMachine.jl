Metronome

The metronome (button Metronom in the header) opens in its own window. It plays through the same audio output as the drum machine but independently of the song: it can run alone or alongside a playing groove (the two are not synchronized). Closing the window stops it. All settings are saved and come back the next time the window is opened.

Display

  • The large number is the current tempo in BPM (quarter notes per minute).
  • One dot per pulse of the bar. Larger dots with an orange ring mark the start of an accent group, the largest one is the one. The dot of the pulse being heard lights up.
  • Below: the bar number, stumm – weiterzählen! (silent – keep counting) in a muted bar of the gap training, and the target tempo while the tempo trainer is active.

ControlEffect
▶ Start / ■ Stopstart on the one / stop (also the space bar)

Song-Tempotakes over the tempo of the song in the main window

Tempo changes take effect immediately, all other settings at the start of the next bar.

Time signature

Taktart (time signature): 1/4 to 7/4 and 3/8 to 12/8. The BPM always count quarter notes; in x/8 time signatures each pulse is an eighth note, so 6/8 at 60 BPM has six eighths at 120 per minute.

Time signatures with groups are accented accordingly, for example 7/8 (2+2+3) or 7/8 (3+2+2), 5/4 (3+2) or 5/4 (2+3), 6/8 (3+3), 12/8 (3+3+3+3).

Subdivision

Unterteilung (subdivision) adds softer clicks between the pulses:

SettingClicks per pulse
keinenone
2 pro Schlag (Achtel)2, eighths in x/4
3 pro Schlag (Triolen)3, triplets
4 pro Schlag (16tel)4, sixteenths in x/4
5 pro Schlag5, quintuplets
6 pro Schlag (Sextolen)6, sextuplets
Swing (1. + 3. Triole)1st and 3rd triplet, the swing feel
Punktiert (1. + 4. 16tel)1st and 4th sixteenth, dotted eighth + sixteenth

Unterteilung leiser/lauter sets the level of these clicks relative to the pulses.

Accents

Betonung (accents) decides which pulses sound:

SettingEffect
Akzent auf der 1accent on the one, group starts slightly accented, subdivision on
Ohne Akzentall pulses equal, subdivision on
Nur 2 und 4only the even pulses, as for swing and backbeat practice
Nur Offbeats (+)only the "and" between the pulses
Nur die 1only the first pulse of each bar

The subdivision only sounds with the first two settings.

Sound

Klang (sound): Klick, Beep, Woodblock, Cowbell, Claves, Sticks (synthesized), or instruments of the kit selected in the main window: Kit: Side Stick, Kit: Ride (accent on the ride bell) and Kit: Hi-Hat. The kit sounds follow a kit change in the main window.

Lautstärke (volume) sets the overall level of the metronome.

Gap training

Lücken-Training: spielen n Takte, stumm m Takte (gap training: play n bars, mute m bars): the metronome plays n bars and then stays silent for m bars, over and over. Keep the time during the silent bars; if you are still on the one when the click returns, the tempo held. 0 silent bars switches it off. Choosing silent bars while the play count is 0 sets it to 4.

Tempo trainer

Tempo-Trainer: + x BPM alle n Takte bis max BPM: after every n bars the tempo rises by x BPM until the maximum is reached; then it stays there. 0 switches it off. The slider and the display follow the rising tempo, stopping keeps the reached tempo.

Keys

The keys work while no input field in the metronome window is being edited.

Delay

The click is heard after the audio buffer (audio_latency, 0.08 s by default, see the README). The display is delayed by the same amount so that the lit dot matches what is heard.
