# Changelog

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-20)

- Added: *Play* can send your pattern or song to the e-drum module instead of the computer speakers (menu *Ausgang*). You hear it in the headphones of the module and play along.
- Added slider *Pegel* to make that playback quieter than your own playing.
- Added: the click of the module follows the tempo of DrumMachine (TD-27: set Sync Mode to EXTERNAL).

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-20)

- Changed *● Aufnahme*: records what you play into a new song, bar by bar. Tempo, grid, time signature and length are set in a window, the take ends when you stop playing.
- Added *Einmessen*: you play a short groove along and DrumMachine sets loud and soft for each pad and the timing.
- Added a second tom track, 12 tracks.
- Added *Human*: small differences in loudness and timing, so a groove sounds less mechanical.
- Added a log file for bug reports, its path is in the *Info* window.
- Added a setting for your own song folder.
- Changed: an open window comes to the front when its button is pressed again.
- Fixed uneven loudness in kits with many recordings.
- Fixed the beat display of the metronome window.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-19)

- Added recording from an e-drum kit by USB, made for the Roland TD-27 (*● Aufnahme*, menu *MIDI*).
- Added *Mithören*: your hits also play the sound of the chosen kit.
- Added MIDI export, e.g. for a DAW or MuseScore.
- Added MIDI files in the setup player, played by the drum module.
- Changed the *Docs* window: one button per document, long sections split into pages.
- Fixed ornaments on the first and last step in exports, the echo effect and restarting the audio after a device error.
- Fixed about 30 smaller errors.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-18)

First version in the Julia registry.

- Added the metronome window with time signatures, subdivisions, accents and tempo trainer.
- Added the setup player for the stage: songs and WAV files in order with pauses and countdown beeps, and a setup editor.
- Added ornaments per note: double stroke, buzz roll, flam, drag.
- Added an own tempo per pattern, up to 24 patterns per song, 32nd grid and tempos up to 400 BPM.
- Added nine free kits, downloaded when chosen in the kit menu.
- Added the import of grooves from browser drum machines.
- Added track volume in dB and L⇄R per track.
- Added presets for drum and bass, jungle, marches and more.
- Added: the last session is restored at start.
